## Working remotely, safely
tipo: portada

> Working from home gives us flexibility. It also gives criminals new ways in.
> In the next few minutes you'll see the habits that keep you and Calder safe.

## Where incidents start
tipo: grafico

- Home or public networks: 18
- **Unmanaged devices**: 11
- Fake IT support messages: 6
- Other causes: 3

> Last year we handled thirty-eight incidents related to remote work.
> {1} Eighteen started on home or public networks.
> {2} Eleven on devices that Calder doesn't manage, like a family laptop.
> {3} Six with fake IT support messages.
> {4} And only three had other causes.

## "My home Wi-Fi is safe"
tipo: mito
mito: My home Wi-Fi is private, so I don't need to worry about it.
realidad: A router with the **default password** is one of the easiest ways in.

> Many people think their home network is safe just because it's at home.
> {1} In reality, a router that still uses its default password is one of the easiest ways in. Change it, and make sure it uses WPA2 or WPA3.

## Public Wi-Fi in three steps
tipo: proceso

- **Connect the VPN**: open GlobalConnect before anything else.
- **Check the lock icon**: GlobalConnect must show "Connected".
- **No VPN? Use your phone**: switch to your mobile hotspot.

> In a café, hotel or airport, follow three steps.
> {1} First, connect the Calder VPN, GlobalConnect, before you open anything work-related.
> {2} Then check that it really says connected.
> {3} And if the VPN won't connect, don't risk it: use your phone's hotspot instead.

## Your laptop, your responsibility
tipo: comparar

- [si] Lock your screen every time you step away.
- [no] Let family members use your work laptop.
- [si] Install updates within 24 hours.
- [no] Open work email on a personal tablet.

> Some habits make all the difference.
> {1} Lock your screen every time you step away, even at home.
> {2} Never let family or friends use your work laptop.
> {3} Install updates within a day.
> {4} And never open work email on a personal device, not even just this once.

## Summary
tipo: cierre

- Lock your screen and keep your laptop **just for work**.
- On public Wi-Fi, **VPN first**, or use your hotspot.
- Nobody from IT will **ask for your password or code**.

> That's it. Keep your laptop just for work, use the VPN on public networks, and never share your password or code.
